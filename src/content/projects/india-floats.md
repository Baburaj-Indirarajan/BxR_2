IF is a contemporary office interior designed to feel confident, warm, and memorable.

The design brings together timber ceilings, softly illuminated wall panels, marble surfaces, brass accents, and generous planting. Each space is composed as a calm backdrop for work, conversation, and brand expression.

From the executive office to the lounge and reception moments, the interiors use consistent material language and carefully controlled lighting to create a workplace with its own identity.
