PM is a contemporary residence shaped by light, proportion, and a close relationship with its surroundings.

The design brings together warm material surfaces, generous openings, and carefully composed rooms that move naturally between private retreat and shared family life.

Every space is resolved with a quiet material language, allowing the architecture, landscape, and everyday rituals of the home to work together.
