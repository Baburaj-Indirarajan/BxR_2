TN is a grand residence shaped by light, proportion, and an appreciation for crafted detail.

The project moves between generous living rooms, intimate private spaces, and a landscaped exterior. Classical arches and ornate metalwork are balanced with contemporary planning, comfortable furniture, and carefully controlled lighting.

From the arrival court to the grand stair and garden pavilion, every setting is composed as part of one warm, expressive family home.
