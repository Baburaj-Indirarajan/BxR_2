VV is a contemporary home designed around light, proportion, and a sense of calm.

The residence brings together robust architectural surfaces, warm timber, natural stone, and generous openings. Each room is connected to the next through a sequence of framed views and carefully considered transitions.

From the arrival experience to the private living spaces, the design balances shelter and openness to create a home that feels grounded, comfortable, and enduring.
